To Buy or To Rent

Find out which option is better for your wallet.

An Android app that compares buying a UK home with renting and investing the difference, using your own numbers and average mortgage rates published by the Bank of England. No account, no ads and no tracking: your numbers stay on your phone.

It is a calculator, not financial advice.
